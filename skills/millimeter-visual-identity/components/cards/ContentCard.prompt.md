The default container for copy, stats or a chart. Neutral, lets its content lead.

```jsx
<ContentCard radius="lg">
  <Kicker index="02">TONE STACK</Kicker>
  <Body>Three steps only. A fourth level of hierarchy uses an accent border instead.</Body>
</ContentCard>
```

- Bigger card, bigger radius. 12px for a chip-sized card, 20px for a half-screen panel.
- Never add a shadow, a glow, or a coloured strip on one edge.
