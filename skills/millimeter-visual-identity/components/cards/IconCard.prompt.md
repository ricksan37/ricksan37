Icon card: glyph in its fixed accent over a dense grid field, Inter Semibold label centred below.

```jsx
<IconCard name="database" label="Ingestion" />
<IconCard name="pipeline" label="Orchestration" />
```

- Use in grids of three or four to fill a media zone.
- The glyph keeps its concept hue. Do not tint a whole row to one accent.
