Numbered card for the left rail and index grids.

```jsx
<NavCard number="01" label="Palette" tone="blue" active />
<NavCard number="02" label="Type" tone="amber" />
```

- The accent is the border and the number. The fill stays Slate. This is the key inversion from the light system.
- Rail hues are fixed by position: 01 blue, 02 amber, 03 vermilion, 04 green.
