Use when a single number beats any chart. Three or four of these make the KPI row of a dashboard.

```jsx
<StatCard value="38%" label="Share of ingest" tone="blue" delta="+4.2 pts" />
```

- Colour the number, never the card. Stat cards are never accent-filled and never violet.
- Delta sign drives the hue: positive Green, negative Vermilion. Never invert that.
