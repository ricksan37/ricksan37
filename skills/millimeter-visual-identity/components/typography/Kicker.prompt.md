Mono uppercase kicker in an accent colour. Use it to open every content slide, card group, or document section.

```jsx
<Kicker index="01" tone="blue">PALETTE</Kicker>
```

- `index` renders `01 PALETTE`. Index, space, label. This system uses no dash, not even in kickers.
- Tone follows the screen's lead accent. Do not mix two kicker tones on one screen.
- 11px, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.14em`. Never scale it up to headline size.
