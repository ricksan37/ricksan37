Archivo Black heading at one of five size steps, always tracked to -0.02em.

```jsx
<Display level="display">Depth comes from tone</Display>
<Display level="h2">Grounds and ink</Display>
```

- Keep the size jump to body copy dramatic. A timid contrast is how this system gets diluted.
- `tone="accent"` is for rare one-word emphasis. Headlines are Paper by default.
- Centre display titles only. Never centre body copy.
